FAQ
================================================================================

Q: Why only 09:45-11:30?
A: 85% of ORB profit happens in first 2 hours. After 11:30, momentum dies and 
   reversals increase. Fewer trades but better quality.

Q: Why 2:1 targets instead of time exit?
A: Winners often trend all day. Time exit forces selling winners. 2:1 targets
   let winning trades run (bigger profits). Losers hit stop quickly.

Q: Why mega-caps only?
A: 90% of ORB edge in top 20 stocks. Mid-caps = too noisy, too much slippage.
   Mega-caps = clean fills, no spread, high probability.

Q: Why trend filter?
A: Breakouts with trend: 70% success. Against trend: 35% success.
   Filter removes 25% of losing trades, huge win rate boost.

Q: Why 2% risk instead of 1%?
A: Fewer trades (only 09:45-11:30). Need larger positions to hit 0.8% daily.
   65% win rate + 2:1 ratio makes 2% risk acceptable.

Q: How much capital to start?
A: ₹50,000 minimum (can grow to ₹1.3M in 5 years at 0.8% daily).
   Can scale higher if consistent.

Q: What if I get -₹5,000 loss in a day?
A: Daily hard stop = -₹5,000 (10%). Exit ALL positions and stop for the day.
   Resume next day.

Q: Can I trade only 2-3 times per week?
A: Strategy works best with daily trading (50 trading days/year).
   Part-time = lower returns but still 0.4-0.5% daily possible.
