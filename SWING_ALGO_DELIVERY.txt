📊 STRATEGY SPECIFICATION (IMPLEMENTED)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SETUP A: PULLBACK TO EMA20
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Entry Criteria:
  • Low <= EMA20 × 1.005 (pullback to fast MA)
  • Close > EMA20 (bullish close)
  • Bullish rejection candle (hammer OR engulfing)

Order Execution:
  • Trigger: High × 1.001 (buy-stop at market)
  • Stop: Swing low × 0.999
  • Target1: Prior swing high

Risk Validation:
  • Risk/Reward >= 1.5
  • Max stop <= 2.5 × ATR
  • Valid for 2 trading days


SETUP B: BREAKOUT WITH RISING ADX
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Entry Criteria:
  • Close > EMA50 and close > resistance level
  • ADX > 20 (strong trend)
  • ADX rising vs 5 bars ago (momentum building)
  • Volume > 1.5 × 30-day average

Order Execution:
  • Trigger: High × 1.001 (buy-stop)
  • Stop: Breakout base low × 0.999
  • Target1: Longer-term swing high

Risk Validation:
  • Risk/Reward >= 1.5
  • Max stop <= 2.5 × ATR


POSITION MANAGEMENT (DAILY)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Entry (Next Day):
  • Check: open > trigger + 0.5×ATR? → Cancel (no chasing)
  • Gap down model: Fill at min(open, trigger)

Stop Loss:
  • Market exit if close <= stop
  • GTT stop-loss order placed immediately on fill

Exits:
  1. Partial at target1: Sell 50%, move stop to breakeven
  2. Remainder: Exit when close < EMA20
  3. ADX deceleration (Setup B): If ADX peaks and turns down + price breaks structure
  4. Time stop: Exit after 20+ days

Portfolio Guards:
  • Max 5 open positions
  • Max 2 per sector
  • Max 5% total open risk
  • Halt new entries if monthly DD <= -6%

⚡ CRITICAL DESIGN DECISIONS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ PLUG-IN ARCHITECTURE
   New strategies inherit from Strategy base class and override scan()/manage()
   Easy to add new strategies without touching backtester

✅ REALISTIC COSTS
   Every charge modeled: STT, DP, exchange, slippage, tax + margin
   Costs deducted from P&L before metrics calculation

✅ NO LOOK-AHEAD
   Backtest replicates real-world: Signal T, Order T+1, Fill T+1
   No future data leakage

✅ PARAMETER IN YAML
   All config in single YAML file
   Secrets (API keys) in environment variables
   No hardcoded values

✅ GEMINI REPORTS ONLY
   Gemini used ONLY for daily reports and event screening
   NEVER for signal generation (humans decide, AI reports)

✅ SINGLE CODEBASE FOR BACKTEST & LIVE
   Same strategy.scan() and strategy.manage() used in backtest and live
   No separate "backtester code" vs "live code" - they're identical

✅ PORTFOLIO GUARDS
   Sector limits, position counts, open risk caps
   Monthly drawdown halt at -6%
   Repeated order rejection monitoring
